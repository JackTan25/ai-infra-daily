### [1] [Falcons: Pushing the Speed-Ratio Limits of Lossy Compression for Parallel Scientific Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41209)
*Bing Lu,Jinyang Liu,Xinyang Ma,Quantong Fu,Chenwei Hou,Zedong Liu,Wenjing Huang,Yida Gu,Xingchen Liu,Zheng Wei,Anbang Wu,Xiaofeng Hou,Chao Li,Guangming Tan,Minyi Guo,Dingwen Tao*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: Falcons 是一个可广泛部署的误差有界有损压缩器，通过内存规整化插值 + SIMD 向量化、分布自适应无损编码、以及面向多种并行化方案的应用级优化，在保持高压缩比的同时实现超高速吞吐，显著降低百亿亿次（exa-scale）科学计算中的数据压缩开销。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 科学数据体量在 exa-scale 计算下急剧膨胀，误差有界有损压缩因高压缩比与可控保真度成为必需手段。但现有压缩器为达到足够压缩比，计算开销过大，成为真实并行科学应用中的性能瓶颈（压缩/解压吞吐跟不上 I/O 与计算速度，压缩本身反而成为新瓶颈）。作者目标是同时拿到高压缩比与极高吞吐，把压缩开销压到可忽略，并能适配多种并行化部署方式。

Result: 在顶级超算上的评测中，Falcons 全面优于 SOTA 压缩器：压缩比最高提升 29%，吞吐最高提升 255%。真实应用案例中：大规模并行数据 I/O 加速最高 44.6%，远程传输加速最高 51.5%，完整 WRF 天气研究与预报工作流获得 1.55× 端到端加速。

To overcome the performance bottlenecks of error-bounded lossy compression, we present \textbf{Falcons}, a broadly deployable error-bounded lossy compressor that simultaneously achieves \textbf{high compression ratios with ultra-fast throughput, minimizing the overhead in parallel scientific applications}. Across our evaluations on cutting-edge supercomputers, Falcons clearly outperforms state-of-the-art compressors, simultaneously achieving up to 29\% compression ratio improvement and 255\% throughput improvement. In case studies of real-world applications, Falcons accelerates large-scale parallel data I/O and remote transfer by up to 44.6\% and 51.5\%, and provides 1.55$\times$ end-to-end speedup for the full WRF (Weather Research \& Forecasting) application workflow.

Compared with PICASSO and TorchRec, RecIS reaches up to $2.62\times$ and $1.15\times$ their throughput, respectively, while reducing step latency by up to 61.5\% and 12.9\%. RecIS provides an efficient and scalable training system for large recommender models.

We propose RAMA, a retrieval-augmented multi-agent DRL framework with Approximate Information State (AIS), in which each satellite combines its local observation, recent routing history, neighboring state information, and retrieved entries from a per-satellite episodic memory. With weights frozen in a 140-satellite Kepler constellation, RAMA reduces mean end-to-end latency by 13.4% and 99th-percentile latency by 66.7% relative to the state-of-theart double deep Q-network (DDQN) routing method, mainly by avoiding long detours. Updated online, RAMA remains stable while DDQN degrades. On the 1,584-satellite Starlink shell 1, the reduction in mean latency grows to 93.2%, and updating RAMA online lowers its mean latency by a further 25.5%. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of retrieval-augmented memory for scalable decentralized routing in LEO satellite networks.
