### [1] [DMuon: Scaling Matrix Optimizers in Data Parallelism with Topology-Aware Matrix Ownership and Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07084)
*Starrick Liu,Ruizhi Chengze,Lufang Chen,Michael Zhao,Ran Yu,Xiaofan Li,dance kuzi,Yue Zhang,Zijuan Lin,SongAo Han,YongHua Chen,Ruyi Gan,Hao Wang*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: DMuon 是一个面向 PyTorch FSDP（ZeRO-2/3）与 HSDP 的"矩阵粒度"优化器运行时，通过为每个权重矩阵指定唯一 owner 并在 owner 上完成完整矩阵的 Newton–Schulz（NS）迭代与优化器更新，配合按计算代价的负载均衡、跨副本全局所有权、参数就绪即发布，以及按数据就绪调度两级网络上的稀疏变长集合通信，在 256×H100 上使矩阵优化器（Muon）的训练吞吐追平/超过 FSDP2+AdamW（中位数 1.05×），相对 gather-then-replicate Muon 基线中位数吞吐 2.1×。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 矩阵级优化器（如 Muon）对整个权重矩阵做更新（NS 迭代涉及矩阵乘法、转置、范数等整体算子），与数据并行运行时按元素/按行分片的参数布局天然冲突：一旦参数也被分片（ZeRO-3/FSDP），矩阵优化器要么需要额外的 all-gather 通信，要么在各 rank 上重复计算（降低有效算力利用并放大显存与状态副本）。已有 Muon 训练实践常采用 gather-then-replicate 方式，通信与冗余计算开销显著。论文动机是：在不放弃 FSDP/HSDP 显存优势的前提下，让矩阵优化器高效可用，使其实测吞吐不低于成熟且轻量的 AdamW 路径。

Method: 1) 矩阵粒度所有权（matrix ownership）：每个参数矩阵分配唯一 owner rank，由该 owner 持有完整矩阵，执行 NS 迭代与优化器状态更新，避免元素/行分片下的重复计算与中途 all-gather。2) 负载均衡：按矩阵的计算代价（NS 迭代的 FLOPs/形状相关成本）而非参数数量分配 owner，缓解矩阵尺寸差异导致的 straggler。3) HSDP 下全局所有权：owner 跨副本全局唯一，集群仅保留一份梯度与优化器状态（ZeRO-2/3 语义），更新后的参数一旦就绪即跨节点发布（publish-on-ready），与后续前/反向计算重叠，隐藏发布延迟。4) 通信调度：该布局使每个集合通信的数据量稀疏且长度可变，DMuon 在数据就绪时即调度 collective，并按集群两级网络（intra-/inter-node）拓扑路由，分别利用高带宽域内链路与跨节点链路。5) 与 FSDP2 的梯度归约、参数 gather 路径深度整合，用优化的 gather 与 reduce 吸收矩阵优化器额外引入的通信与计算。

Result: 在最多 256 块 H100 上，覆盖 3 个稠密 LLM 与 2 个 VLA 模型：DMuon 的训练吞吐与 PyTorch FSDP2 + AdamW 持平或更优，256 GPU 规模下中位数为 FSDP2+AdamW 的 1.05×；相对 gather-then-replicate 的 Muon 基线，中位数吞吐为其 2.1×。论文指出增益主要来自优化后的参数 gather 与梯度归约，它们吸收了矩阵优化器额外所需的通信与计算。

Conclusion: 矩阵级优化器与分片式数据并行并不矛盾：把"分片单元"从元素/行提升到矩阵粒度，并由全局唯一 owner 承担整体更新，可以把 NS 迭代的重复计算和额外 all-gather 转化为可被调度的稀疏、变长通信，再借助就绪即发布与拓扑感知路由与计算重叠。工程含义：Muon 类优化器可在现有 FSDP2/HSDP 栈上部署而无需牺牲吞吐，较易作为优化器/运行时插件落地；代价是引入 owner 映射、变长 collective 调度与跨副本状态管理的实现复杂度，且收益依赖拓扑感知与重叠质量。未来方向包括与 ZeRO 分片优化器状态、异构矩阵形状自动划分及更大规模跨集群评估的结合。

Abstract: Matrix optimizers such as Muon update whole weight matrices, which conflicts with the element- or row-wise sharding of data-parallel runtimes. Once parameters are sharded as well, matrix optimizers incur extra communication or repeated computation. We present DMuon, a matrix-granular optimizer runtime for PyTorch FSDP (ZeRO-2/3) and HSDP. DMuon assigns each matrix a unique owner, which performs the Newton--Schulz (NS) iteration and optimizer update on the full matrix, and balances the load across owners by computational cost. On HSDP, ownership is global across replicas, so the cluster keeps a single copy of gradients and optimizer state, and updated parameters are published across nodes as soon as they are ready, overlapping subsequent computation. This layout makes every collective sparse and variable in length, so DMuon schedules these collectives as their data becomes ready and routes them along the cluster's two-level network. On up to 256 H100 GPUs, across three dense LLMs and two VLA models, DMuon matches or exceeds the training throughput of PyTorch FSDP2 with AdamW, reaching 1.05$\times$ that of FSDP2 with AdamW at the median on 256 GPUs. The optimized parameter gathering and gradient reduction absorb the extra communication and computation a matrix optimizer requires. Against a gather-then-replicate Muon baseline, DMuon's throughput is 2.1 times as high at the median.

### [2] [NUMA-Torch: Compiler-Driven NUMA-Aware LLM Prefill on Multi-Socket CPUs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07105)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当 LLM 参数量超出片上缓存容量时，多路 CPU 上 prefill 的性能瓶颈从峰值浮点吞吐转向可持续 DRAM 带宽。而现有 PyTorch 编译路径 TorchInductor 在分配权重时不考虑 NUMA 拓扑，忽视内存 bank 与计算核心的亲和性，使得相当比例的权重访问需穿越 socket 间互连（UPI/QPI），显著降低每 socket 的有效可用带宽。已有 NUMA 优化多在运行时/库层面（如 numa-aware allocator、OpenMP 绑核），缺乏在编译器图层面同时处理权重视图切分与内核调度的一致性方案。

Method: 核心思想是 NUMA-aware Tensor Parallelism（NUMA-TP），实现于 TorchInductor：1) 将每个 NUMA socket 视为一个独立、等价的张量并行 rank/分区；2) 在编译期对权重张量沿合适维度做跨 socket 分片（sharding），使每个 socket 的本地内存只持有自身分片，并生成对应的通信/聚合（all-reduce 或 all-gather）语义；3) 通过 CPU affinity 把每个 socket 的线程池与其本地内存分配严格绑定，保证计算 kernel 的线程只访问本地 NUMA 节点的权重分片，消除远端 DRAM 访问；4) 该变换对用户透明，仍沿用标准 PyTorch 编译流程。评测覆盖 GPT-2（117M/345M/762M）、BERT（Small/Large）这类标准 MHA+MLP 结构，以及 Gemma 3 270M（MQA + GeGLU 门控 MLP）以验证对异构算子模式的通用性。

Result: 通过严格强制数据局部性，跨 socket 流量被有效抑制，prefill 阶段加速比：双路 Haswell 最高 2.63x，双路 Cascade Lake 最高 1.87x，四路 Haswell-EX 最高 2.27x。加速主要来自有效 DRAM 带宽的提升而非算力利用；Cascade Lake 上收益低于 Haswell，说明 UPI 互连与内存子系统代际改进会削弱 NUMA 感知带来的相对收益。

Conclusion: 论文表明 NUMA 拓扑感知应作为编译器级的一等公民纳入张量布局与内核调度决策，而非留给运行时分配器；把"张量并行"的抽象从多设备 GPU 迁移到多 socket CPU，是一种低改动、透明且高收益的带宽优化手段。工程启示：对 CPU 侧 LLM 推理/服务，应把 NUMA 亲和性作为与算子融合同等级别的编译优化；收益随 socket 数与互连代际变化，需结合平台拓扑自适应选择分区策略，并关注 all-reduce 通信开销对小模型的侵蚀。

Abstract: For sufficiently large transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs), whose parameters cannot be retained in on-chip caches, prefill throughput on multi-socket CPUs is often limited by sustainable DRAM bandwidth rather than peak floating-point throughput. In the standard PyTorch compilation (i.e., TorchInductor) path, model weights are allocated without considering the Non-Uniform Memory Access (NUMA) topology: specifically, the affinity between memory banks and compute cores is disregarded. This causes a substantial fraction of weight accesses to traverse the inter-socket interconnect, severely degrading the effective memory bandwidth available to each socket. We address this bottleneck through a compiler-level solution that makes TorchInductor NUMA-aware. Treating each NUMA socket as an independent tensor-parallel partition, we introduce NUMA-aware Tensor Parallelism (NUMA-TP). This approach transparently shards weights across sockets and schedules compute kernels such that thread pools and memory allocations remain strictly local to their respective sockets using CPU affinity. We evaluate the approach on GPT-2 (117M, 345M, 762M) and BERT (Small, Large), which exercise standard multi-head attention and MLP, as well as Gemma 3 270M, which combines Multi-Query Attention with a GeGLU-gated MLP. By strictly enforcing data locality, our approach successfully mitigates cross-socket traffic, achieving prefill speedups of up to 2.63x on a dual-socket Haswell platform, up to 1.87x on a dual-socket Cascade Lake platform, and up to 2.27x on a four-socket Haswell-EX platform.

TL;DR: 该论文系统评估了10个<10B参数的开源轻量LLM在意图驱动网络（IBN）中将自然语言意图翻译为可执行SLO的能力，从翻译性能、意图校验、幻觉、能耗、鲁棒性五个维度在716条placement意图和4种提示设置下评测，指出轻量模型可在翻译与幻觉控制上达到可用水平（严格F1 0.943），但意图校验对重述的鲁棒性仍是开放问题。

Motivation: IBN的第一步（NL意图→基于实时网络快照的SLO）越来越依赖LLM，但大模型带来成本与能耗开销，且通常需要多GPU云基础设施。作者提出核心问题：轻量开源LLM能否可靠接管这一环节，并给出可量化的能效与鲁棒性证据。

Method: 构建716条placement意图基准；选取5个模型族共10个<10B参数LLM；4种提示设置（含zero-shot与few-shot reasoning）；沿五个维度评测：翻译质量（严格F1）、意图校验（合法/非法意图拒绝）、幻觉（不受支持的QoS需求）、GPU能耗（Wh/意图）、对多样化表述的鲁棒性。

Result: 最佳配置严格F1达0.943，可在195条非法意图中拒绝多达191条；few-shot reasoning下最强模型幻觉率约1/100，而zero-shot表现明显较差；无推理时单次意图翻译耗GPU能量0.044–0.148 Wh，对较弱模型而言推理带来的额外能耗是划算的；真实重述使16个配置中15个的非法意图拒绝率下降，鲁棒校验成为关键短板。

Conclusion: 轻量开源LLM在翻译与幻觉控制上已接近可部署水平，且能耗可量化为管理闭环中可接受的量级，说明IBN的意图翻译环节未必需要云托管大模型；但表述鲁棒性导致的校验退化意味着需在系统层引入防护机制（如语义一致性校验、检索式网络状态接地、拒绝策略校准）。

Abstract: Intent-based networking (IBN) promises QoS provisioning in which the network derives the configuration from operator-stated goals. Its first step, translating a natural-language intent into enforceable service-level objectives (SLOs) grounded in a live snapshot of the network state, increasingly relies on large language models (LLMs). These large models add cost and energy to the management loop and typically require multi-GPU, cloud-hosted infrastructure. Can lightweight open LLMs take over this step dependably? We answer this question with a five-dimensional evaluation of ten LLMs with <10B parameters from five families, under four prompting setups, on 716 placement intents. We evaluate translation performance, intent validation, hallucination, energy impact, and robustness to diverse phrasing. The best configurations translate intents with a strict F1 of 0.943 and reject up to 191 of 195 invalid intents. With few-shot reasoning, the strongest models hallucinate unsupported QoS requirements in only about one response in a hundred, whereas the zero-shot setup struggles. A translated intent costs 0.044-0.148 Wh of GPU energy without reasoning, and added energy cost for reasoning pays off for the weaker models. Furthermore, we show that realistic rephrasing lowers the rejection of invalid intents in 15 of 16 configurations for the best-performing models, leaving robust intent validation as a key open challenge for reliable IBN. Our code and data are available: https://github.com/ggck43/intent2slo.
