### [1] [TCDA: Tunable Coded Distributed Arrays for Data Availability Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22508)
*Duc A. Tran,Dung Truong,Duy Le*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: TCDA 提出一种可证明抗任意数量 Byzantine 节点的编码数据可用性采样（DAS）网络层：打破 RDA 只能靠全复制保证鲁棒性的限制，用"每节点保留其被分配数据的比例"作为可调旋钮，在 PUT/存储与 GET 成本之间权衡，并在以太坊参数下首次做到满足 attestation 截止时间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DAS 的网络层必须同时满足三件事：在验证者 attestation 截止时间前完成区块分发（PUT）、存储数据、并按请求提供任意分片（GET），且必须对任意数量 Byzantine 节点保持 trustless。唯一已知具备该保证的 RDA 依赖全复制——每个负责某部分数据的小组完整存储该部分，只需组内一个诚实节点即可，但代价是关键路径（通往 finality）上的 PUT 负载随网络规模增长且不可调，导致数据无法在截止前到达验证者。编码是自然解法，但此前不清楚编码层在无界对手下何时仍鲁棒、代价多少、收益多少。

Method: 提出 TCDA 编码层：在只要"诚实节点足够多、且位置任意"的前提下给出可证明的鲁棒性；引入可调参数——每个节点保留其被分配数据的比例，按数据类型分别设定，并随诚实节点数量变化动态调整。该旋钮在 PUT 成本、存储成本与 GET 成本（采样往返次数）之间进行连续权衡。

Result: 以太坊参数下、32 MiB 区块、推荐上行带宽的评测：RDA 在任何网络规模下都无法赶上 attestation 截止时间；TCDA 在 868 至 40,000 个诚实节点区间内均可调参满足截止时间，单节点存储比复制方案少 1.7–32 倍，代价是每个采样需 2.5–7.7 次往返（RDA 约 1 次）。

Conclusion: 编码并非在无界对手下不可用：只要诚实节点足够，编码层可同时获得鲁棒性与可调的成本结构，从而把"复制 vs. 编码"的二元取舍变成可运维的连续调节。系统贡献在于把 DAS 网络层从"必须全复制才能 trustless"的困境中解放出来，用少量额外的 GET 往返换取关键路径上 PUT 负载与存储的大幅下降，使 DAS 在真实以太坊规模与带宽约束下可行。

Abstract: Data availability sampling (DAS) lets blockchain nodes verify a block's data availability by downloading only a few random pieces of it. Its network layer must disseminate each block in time for validators to attest (PUT), store it, and serve any piece on request (GET). A trustless DAS must sustain any number of Byzantine peers. The only known design with this guarantee, Robust Distributed Arrays (RDA), achieves it by full replication: each group of nodes responsible for part of a block stores that part in full, so a single good node per group suffices. The price to pay is a PUT load on the critical path to finality that grows with the network and cannot be tuned, hence the data may not reach validators before the attestation deadline. Coding is the natural remedy, but it was not known when a coded layer remains robust against an unbounded adversary, what it costs, or what it gains. We propose TCDA, a coded layer that is provably robust given enough good nodes, wherever they lie, with a tunable knob: how much of its assigned data each node keeps. Turning the knob trades PUT and storage costs against GET cost. It can be set per data type and adjusted as the good-node population changes. At Ethereum's parameters, our evaluation for a 32 MiB block at the recommended uplink shows that RDA misses the attestation deadline at any network size, whereas TCDA can be tuned to meet it from 868 good nodes up to the 40,000 tested, storing 1.7 to 32 times less per node than replication, at the price of 2.5 to 7.7 round-trips per sample instead of about one.

### [2] [Fast Distributed $(Δ+ 1)$-Edge Coloring in Bipartite Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22690)
*Yann Bourreau,Sebastian Brandt,Ananth Narayanan*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 该论文证明在LOCAL模型中，二部图的(Δ+1)-边染色可确定性O(f(Δ)·log n)轮完成，常数度二部图上达到确定性O(log n)轮，并且在更强的local-palette约束下也成立。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: (Δ+1)-边染色是分布式图算法的经典基准问题。此前最优结果只对一般图成立，确定性需Õ(log^5 n)、随机化需Õ(log^2 n)轮，n依赖仍是瓶颈；作者希望针对二部图结构获得真正的对数级轮数，并研究更强的local-palette版本（顶点只使用与自身度数相关的局部调色板）。

Method: 尚无细节。摘要称结果为确定性、不用随机源，利用二部图的结构性promise；顶点无需知道自身属于二部划分的哪一侧。核心应是某种确定性局部调色板式算法构造，复杂度对Δ指数级、对n仅为对数因子。

Result: 二部n点图(Δ+1)-边染色：确定性O(f(Δ)·log n)轮；常数度二部图为确定性O(log n)轮。同时对更严格的local-palette版本（边{u,v}须用{1,…,max(deg u,deg v)+1}中的颜色）同样成立。此前为该问题的最优结果。

Conclusion: 在二部图上(Δ+1)-边染色确定性算法几乎收敛到理论极限（O(log n)），为分布式边染色提供了紧致上界；由于是确定性且不依赖二部划分信息，更强更稳健，可直接用于构建确定性局部调色板边染色子程序。

Abstract: We show that, on bipartite $n$-vertex graphs in the LOCAL model of distributed computation, $(Δ+ 1)$-edge coloring can be solved deterministically in $O(f(Δ) \log n)$ rounds for some function $f$.

  Our results improve on the previous state-of-the-art bounds (which hold already on general graphs) whose $n$-dependencies are $\widetilde{O}(\log^5 n)$ rounds deterministically and $\widetilde{O}(\log^2 n)$ rounds randomized [Bernshteyn and Dhawan, J.\ Comb.\ Theory 2025].
  In fact, we obtain the aforementioned results already for a more restrictive version of $(Δ+ 1)$-edge coloring that corresponds to a \emph{local-palette} strengthening of Vizing's theorem shown recently to hold [Christiansen, STOC 2023].
  In this version, every edge $\{u, v\}$ has to be colored with a color from $\{1, \dots, \max(\operatorname{deg}(u), \operatorname{deg}(v))+ 1\}$.
  For all of our results, the vertices do not need to know on which side of the bipartition they lie---the structural promise of bipartiteness suffices.

### [3] [Accelerated Dataflow: scaling inference in streaming data analytics with efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22731)

This increases throughput 1.5x to 2x for streaming pipelines using LLMs while maintaining similar latency with up to 75% peak resource savings and 84% lower peak tail latency compared with existing streaming data processing systems.

Each idea is introduced with a figure and closed with worked examples.

Conclusion: 该工作建立了从聚合密度演化反演宏观有向输运的运动学理论基座:用加权最小能量准则消除环流非唯一性,用加权椭圆 Neumann 问题给出可计算形式,并显式刻画了"密度不可辨识"的信息边界。其对工程的意义在于:为移动群体(交通、人群、无人机集群)的仅密度可观测输运估计提供可辨识性诊断与避免病态反演的规范解;为网络化观测与延迟条件下的闭环控制提供质心级低维反馈量;并为离散基础设施与连续流耦合的仿真/调度数字孪生提供建模接口。

Abstract: Classical stochastic geometry primarily addresses forward analysis by prescribing spatial point-process laws and mobility models. It does not directly address the inverse problem of recovering directional macroscopic transport from aggregate density evolution in non-stationary spatial networks. This paper develops the kinematic foundation of fluid-spatiotemporal stochastic geometry (F-STSG), representing an evolving mobile population by an intensity field under an assumed continuum limit while retaining the network infrastructure as a discrete spatial measure. We formulate macroscopic transport reconstruction as an inverse boundary value problem. Under a strictly positive bounded intensity and compatible source and boundary data, an instantaneous weighted minimum-energy criterion selects a unique velocity representative from the admissible class. The reconstruction reduces to a weighted elliptic Neumann problem and separates density-identifiable transport from weighted-solenoidal circulation that is not identifiable from density evolution alone. We further derive the information flux, interface transport, velocity-divergence relation, and centroid dynamics. Numerical experiments examine reconstruction consistency and sensitivity, while the public New Era of Urban Traffic Monitoring with Aerial Footage (pNEUMA) dataset provides held-out validation of internal transport. A separate unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) study evaluates the centroid as a causal downstream control reference under observation delay, motion constraints, and execution perturbations.
