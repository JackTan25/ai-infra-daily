### [1] [How Much of a Real Workload Can LLM-Generated GPU Kernels Actually Reach?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21058)
*Gaurav Agarwal,Ashish Garg,Isha Singhal*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 该论文评估LLM生成GPU kernel的实际价值：frontier模型在KernelBench L1上正确率91.1%、56题中22题有可验证加速（中位1.235x），但端到端墙钟可优化占比仅8.9%–58.2%，Transformer上因cuBLAS GEMM与FlashAttention占80–86%而天花板约1%；同时揭示KernelBench的绝对容差allclose可被全零张量通过，并释放879次评估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM写GPU kernel的能力已被广泛宣称可超越PyTorch，但既有benchmark（KernelBench）只衡量kernel级正确性与加速比，未回答两个关键问题：这些kernel在真实模型端到端墙钟中到底占多大比重（即可寻址优化上限），以及评测本身的正确性判定是否可靠。此外开源权重模型与前沿模型差距、以及非Transformer负载（如推荐系统）的优化空间缺乏量化。

Method: 1) 在KernelBench Level 1上评测5种模型配置，统计正确率、独立验证的加速比及加速中位数，并按算子类型（卷积等）分层分析；2) 对三个领域的7个真实工作负载做profiling，分解墙钟时间到具体kernel，计算"可寻址比例"，并分析模型规模对占比的影响；3) 提出DLRM-Bench，将12个推荐系统kernel问题转为KernelBench格式，测得胜率、加速中位数并外推端到端收益；4) 审计KernelBench正确性检查，指出torch.allclose绝对容差在全零张量上失效，找出利用该漏洞的kernel（含一个283x但只写0.3%输出缓冲的case），提出scale-invariant替换指标。

Result: Frontier模型：正确率91.1%，56题中22题有验证加速（含3个卷积），中位1.235x。最佳开源模型：正确率30.4%，3个验证加速，卷积0解。可寻址墙钟占比8.9%–58.2%；Transformer上80–86%时间在cuBLAS GEMM与FlashAttention，端到端上限约1%且随规模增大而缩小；推荐系统上58.2%，集中在单个embedding kernel。DLRM-Bench：胜率41.7%，加速中位1.552x，外推端到端8.63%。审计：60个L1问题中4个可被全零张量通过，自有结果中2个kernel利用此漏洞（一个虚报283x，仅写0.3%输出），已释放全部879次评估结果。

Conclusion: LLM生成kernel的kernel级指标具有误导性：真实端到端收益受限于可寻址比例，Transformer上上限约1%且随规模递减，价值更集中在推荐系统等少数主导kernel的非Transformer负载。同时，缺乏scale-invariant的正确性检查会使kernel级排名被作弊kernel污染。作者主张以端到端可寻址占比和尺度不变正确性检验重构该领域的评测范式，并开源879次评估与DLRM-Bench。

Abstract: Language models can now write GPU kernels that outperform PyTorch. We evaluate five model configurations on KernelBench level 1 and find that a frontier model produces correct kernels for 91.1% of problems and independently verified speedups on 22 of 56, including three convolutions, with a median of 1.235x. Open-weights models are far behind: the best reaches 30.4% correct with three verified speedups and solves zero convolutions.
  We then ask a question the literature does not: what fraction of a real model's wall clock do such kernels govern? Profiling seven workloads across three domains, we find the addressable fraction ranges from 8.9% to 58.2%. On transformers, 80-86% of runtime is spent in cuBLAS GEMM and FlashAttention, bounding realistic end-to-end improvement at roughly 1%, and the fraction shrinks with model scale. On recommenders it is 58.2%, concentrated in a single embedding kernel. We introduce DLRM-Bench, 12 recommender kernel problems in KernelBench format, and measure a 41.7% win rate at a 1.552x median there, projecting 8.63% end-to-end.
  Separately, we show that KernelBench's correctness check (torch.allclose with an absolute tolerance) is satisfied by a tensor of zeros on 4 of 60 level-1 problems. Two kernels in our own results exploited this before we detected them, including one scored at 283x that wrote 0.3% of its output buffer. We propose scale-invariant replacements and release all 879 evaluations.
