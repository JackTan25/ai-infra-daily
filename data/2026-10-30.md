### [1] [ConfluxServe: Reconciling Resource Utilization and Parallelism Efficiency across Prefill and Decode in LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37810)
*Wenhao Chen,Hong Zhang*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 提出 Conflux Parallelism (XP)：一种允许同一 batch 内 prefill 与 decode 使用不同并行配置的并行执行抽象，基于算子级 Transformer 结构构造异构执行路径；并实现 SLO 感知的推理服务系统 ConfluxServe，在满足 TTFT/TBT 约束下最大化集群 goodput，实测较最强基线提升 1.26–1.62x。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Prefill 与 decode 在资源使用上互补（compute-bound vs memory/bandwidth-bound），但并行偏好不同（prefill 偏张量并行、decode 偏更细粒度切分或数据并行）。现状存在两难：分离执行（disaggregation/phase-specific execution）保留各自最优并行度，却导致互补资源闲置；co-batching（如 chunked prefill 混合批）提升利用率，但要求统一并行配置，损害执行效率。核心问题即 "utilization-efficiency dilemma"（利用率-效率权衡）。

Method: 关键洞察是利用 Transformer 的算子级结构：batch 内不同请求的并行拓扑只需在需要交互的算子（如 attention、collective 通信）处对齐，其余算子（MLP、norm、linear 等）可各自采用不同的 batching 与并行策略。据此，XP 在同一 batch 内为 prefill token 和 decode token 构造异构执行路径（heterogeneous execution path），在算子粒度上切换并行配置。上层 ConfluxServe 是 SLO-aware 调度系统：以 TTFT 与 TBT 为约束，联合决定批次组成、各算子并行策略与资源分配，目标是集群级 goodput 最大化。

Result: 在生产负载上，ConfluxServe 相比最强基线将 serving goodput 提升 1.26–1.62x。

Conclusion: 该工作把"并行配置"从整个批次/整个阶段的粗粒度决策下沉到算子粒度，提出了一种介于 PD 分离与 co-batching 之间的第三条路线，在保持资源互补利用的同时避免统一并行配置带来的效率损失。对工程实践的意义：为 PD 混合调度提供了更细的调度自由度，可与 chunked prefill、PD disaggregation、EP/TP 组合策略叠加；但代价是执行引擎需支持算子级异构通信组与动态并行切换，增加了 collective 同步、KV 布局与图捕获/编译（CUDA Graph、kernel 选择）的复杂度。

Abstract: Prefill and decode are complementary in resource usage, but often divergent in parallelism preferences. Executing the two phases separately preserves their preferred parallelism but leaves complementary resources underutilized, while co-batching improves utilization but often requires a uniform parallel configuration that hurts execution efficiency.
  To resolve this utilization-efficiency dilemma, we propose Conflux Parallelism (XP), a parallel execution abstraction that co-executes prefill and decode with different parallelism within the same batch. Conflux Parallelism exploits operator-level Transformer structure to construct a heterogeneous execution path with different batching and parallelism strategies across operators, combining the best of co-batching and phase-specific execution. Building on Conflux Parallelism, we design ConfluxServe, an SLO-aware serving system that maximizes cluster goodput under TTFT and TBT constraints. Evaluation on production workloads shows that ConfluxServe improves serving goodput by 1.26-1.62x over the strongest baselines.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FFT本身的并行化已高度成熟，但STFT作为“加窗+多次FFT”的复合算子，其并行实现的性能特征（可扩展性、内存、通信成本、计算/通信瓶颈）缺乏系统性认识；而STFT是频谱分析、声学前端、以及大量信号处理与科学计算负载的核心组件，工程上需要知道在不同并发度与并行范式下该选哪种实现。

Conclusion: 论文给出并行STFT两大算法族的“定标性特征”：内存效率 vs. 低并发运行时/串行分数之间的权衡，可作为STFT类信号处理流水线在并行后端选型与调优（并发度、范式、通信占比）的工程依据。局限在于属于性能表征与实现分析，未提出新的渐近算法改进，也未涉及GPU/加速器上的向量化与批处理实现。

While the Fourier transform algorithms, particularly the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithm, has been well developed to take advantage of parallel computing, there is limited work on understanding the performance of parallel short-time Fourier transform algorithms. In this study, two parallel short-time Fourier transform algorithms, one is FFT-based and the other a quasi-parallel feedforward algorithm, are implemented and analyzed in comparison with each other. It is found that while the quasi-parallel algorithm outperforms in runtime under low concurrency settings, the FFT-based algorithm scales better and has lower memory usage. For each algorithm, three parallel approaches, shared-memory, distributed-memory and hybrid distributed-shared-memory, are tested. Profiling results show that all implementations are compute bound except for the hybrid feedforward algorithm, and that the distributed-memory variants incur significantly higher communication cost. Overall, this study presents a deeper and more comprehensive analysis and demonstrates the defining characteristics of different families of parallel short-time Fourier transform algorithms, in which the FFT-based approach uses less memory while the quasi-parallel feedforward algorithm has lower runtime but higher serial fraction.

### [3] [Random Centres and the $\varepsilon$-Dependence of Constant-Round Planar Domination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38020)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 前作（arXiv:2610.09618）证明平面图确定性常数轮LOCAL算法逼近MDS的下确界为7，但其 (7+ε)-近似需要 T0+O((c0/ε)^κ) 轮、κ≈25.81，指数过大；作者要回答该指数能否显著降低，并刻画对 ε 的依赖是否为最优。

On forests and on outerplanar graphs, with no bound on the degree, $O(1/\varepsilon)$ rounds suffice, so there the optimal number of rounds is $Θ(1/\varepsilon)$. The planar level bounds, the bounds on paths and the deterministic bound on forests are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib.

### [4] [The Endgame of FSDP Communication: Layout-Aware Collectives on Copy Engines, RDMA, and NVSwitch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38228)
*Zejian Wang,Yifan Zhao,Kaihui Ren,Xiaoling Zhou,Yubin Wang,Chenhao Guan,Lin Zhang,Yufeng Li,Zhikang Mo,Zhuoru Ma,Yingtong Xiong,Jihu Guo,Yiqiao Liao,Chengru Song*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: TIDE 是一套基于公开 NCCL 2.31+ API 的零拷贝集合通信实现，通过把 FSDP2 的按参数张量布局折叠进集合通信寻址，消除 all-gather 输出到参数存储、梯度打包到扁平 fp32 缓冲的两次布局转换拷贝，并大幅降低通信对并发 GEMM 的干扰。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FSDP2 的参数/梯度按 per-parameter 张量存储，而集合通信路径使用 flat、rank-major 的大缓冲，二者布局不一致导致：(1) all-gather 输出需拷贝进参数存储、梯度需打包进整组 fp32 缓冲，在 8K tokens/GPU 的 Llama-3.1 70B 训练中布局转换占 step 时间 6.4%；(2) 默认 NCCL reduce-scatter 会占用 SM，使本可单波完成的并发 GEMM 慢 69%。目标是在不改变 FSDP2 存储与计算 kernel 的前提下消除这两类开销。

Method: 核心思想是"布局即寻址"：保留 FSDP2 的 per-parameter 存储和计算 kernel，把布局放置折叠进集合通信的地址计算。TIDE-AG 用 NVSwitch multicast 把分片直接投递到参数存储，跨节点用节流的 recursive-doubling RDMA，传输与放置全程不占用 SM。TIDE-RS 直接消费 per-parameter bf16 梯度并归约为 fp32 分片（NCCL 不提供的混合精度能力），节点内用 copy engine 加短生命周期 CTA 做累积、跨节点用 NVSwitch 归约；采用两阶段流水把梯度读取与网络推进解耦，可选 Slot-Reuse 在已腾空的 all-gather slot 中暂存梯度。全部构建于公开 NCCL API，不使用私有接口。

Result: 64 GPU 上，相对 Stock FSDP2 最多降低 step 时间 27.1%，相对 FSDP2+SymmMem 降低 6.0%（Llama-3.1 70B、Wan2.2、Qwen3-30B-A3B）；reduce-scatter 的 SM 时间降低 12.5–14.5 倍，对并发计算的干扰降低最多一个数量级；固定每 GPU 输出大小时，reduce-scatter 缓冲区从 16 到 64 GPU 近乎恒定；峰值内存比 FSDP2+SymmMem 低最多 4.7 GiB，在 Wan2.2 上配合 Slot-Reuse 甚至低于 Stock FSDP2。

Conclusion: TIDE 表明，训练框架的通信层可以通过"布局折叠进寻址 + 绕过 SM 的数据搬运"获得显著收益，而不必改动上层存储抽象或计算 kernel。其工程影响在于：一是把 NCCL 从"扁平缓冲"抽象中解耦，使框架侧张量容器可直接作为通信目标，削减框架↔通信库的胶水拷贝；二是用 copy engine/少量短 CTA 替代 SM 密集的 reduce-scatter，直接改善通信-计算重叠质量；三是混合精度 bf16→fp32 归约与近恒定显存缓冲区降低了大规模扩展时的显存与带宽压力。局限在于依赖 NVSwitch/NVLink 拓扑与 NCCL 2.31+ 新 API，跨代硬件与异构网络下的可移植性、以及 NVSwitch multicast 在拥塞下的公平性仍需验证。

Abstract: Fully Sharded Data Parallelism (FSDP2) maintains parameters and gradients as per-parameter tensors, whereas its collective path operates on flat, rank-major buffers. Converting between these layouts copies all-gather outputs into parameter storage and packs gradients into full-group fp32 buffers. Layout conversion alone accounts for 6.4% of step time in Llama-3.1 70B training at 8K tokens per GPU. Communication kernels impose a second cost: a default NCCL reduce-scatter can slow a concurrent GEMM that would otherwise complete in a single wave by 69%. We present TIDE, a set of zero-copy collectives built on public NCCL 2.31+ APIs. TIDE preserves FSDP2's per-parameter storage and compute kernels while folding layout placement into collective addressing. TIDE-AG delivers shards directly to parameter storage using NVSwitch multicast and paced recursive-doubling RDMA, with no SMs used for transport or placement. TIDE-RS consumes per-parameter bf16 gradients directly and reduces them into fp32 shards (a mixed-precision mode that NCCL does not provide) using copy engines and short-lived thread blocks (CTAs) for node-local accumulation and NVSwitch for cross-node reduction. ### [5] [Only Accepted Tokens Count: An Energy Accounting of Speculative Decoding Under Latency Targets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40287)
*Bole Ma,Ayesha Afzal,Jan Eitzinger,Harald Koestler,Gerhard Wellein*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 该文系统测量了生产级 serving 条件（SGLang + CUDA graph，H100/H200，batch≤64，5 个语料）下投机解码（speculative decoding）对「每 token 能耗」的影响，指出投机解码的节能并非普适收益，而强烈依赖接受率、batch 大小、attention 家族与文本域，且在大 batch 下会因验证阶段从访存受限转为计算受限而由「省」变「亏」。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 在生产级 serving 栈上做端到端能耗测量：backend 为 SGLang 并启用 CUDA graphs，硬件为 H100 与 H200，batch size 扫描至 64，覆盖 5 个语料。对比不同 target 模型家族、不同 drafter 方案（含 block drafter 与低接受率 drafter），按 draft/verify 阶段拆解能耗，并将验证阶段放在 arithmetic intensity 与 roofline 视角下分析其处于访存受限还是计算受限区间；同时考察延迟目标收紧与 DVFS 余量的交互。

Result: 在 dense 模型上，能耗主导项是验证而非 drafting。节能随 batch 增大而收缩，因为 batching 已经把投机解码本要用接受 token 去分摊的权重读取摊薄了。大 batch 下 block drafter 把验证推过算术/访存平衡点进入计算受限区，节能变为亏损；低接受率 drafter 直接抬高每 token 能耗。接受率存在系统性差异：所有被评估的 drafter 在对话上接受的 token 都少于代码，所有训练过的 drafter 在低资源语言 Romansh 上都少于英语。用投机解码满足更紧的延迟目标会额外增加能耗；不投机时，加大 batch 本身就能降低能耗/token。投机只在接受率高时才叠加到这份节能上，且在所测模型上几乎没有给延迟保持型 DVFS 留下余量。

Abstract: Large language model serving is limited by facility power. Energy per token determines how many tokens a datacenter can serve, while a latency target bounds how quickly each user receives them. During decode, every weight is read from memory for every generated token. Batching amortizes this read across users but makes no individual user faster. Speculative decoding does: a drafter proposes several future tokens, and the target model verifies them in a single forward pass that reuses the same weight read. Because every drafted token must be verified whether or not it is accepted, the energy speculation saves depends on how many drafted tokens are accepted. This saving is hard to pin down. It varies across target models from different attention families and across drafting schemes, and it depends on the text being served. Every drafter we evaluate accepts fewer tokens on conversation than on code, and every trained drafter accepts fewer on Romansh, a low-resource language, than on English. We measure speculative decoding under production serving conditions (SGLang, CUDA graphs enabled) on H100 and H200 GPUs, at batch sizes up to 64 across 5 corpora. On dense models, verification, not drafting, dominates the energy cost. The saving shrinks as the batch grows, because batching has already shared the weight read that speculation would divide among the accepted tokens. At large batch sizes, block drafters push verification past the balance point between arithmetic and memory traffic into the compute-bound regime, where the saving becomes a loss, and a drafter with low acceptance raises energy per token. Meeting a tighter latency target with speculation then costs additional energy. Larger batches lower energy per token without speculation.
