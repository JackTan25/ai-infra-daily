### [1] [Cambium: Participant-Built Commitment Roots for Shared Digital Infrastructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30409)
*Mansoor "Manny" Ahmed,Dann Toliver,Ekaterina Pavlova*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: Cambium 是一个许可制 P2P 承诺服务：每个周期由全体参与者共同构建承诺根，用定长委员会认证输入并采用递归倍增合并，使无单方决定根内容、每节点 O(c log(n/c)) 消息、O(log(n/c)) 存储证明，并在 8–256 节点 AWS Go 原型上实现 12–148 ms 周期完成、<1.5 ms 验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有承诺服务（时间戳、透明日志、来源登记）要么由单一运营方控制（存在信任与审查问题），要么用区块链以全网对每次写入达成共识，代价过高。目标是去掉单一运营方，同时避免全网共识的开销：让参与者在每个周期共同构建根，使得没有任何单方决定哪些输入被纳入根。

Method: 许可制 P2P 协议，按周期构建承诺根。1) 划分定长委员会（大小 c）对输入进行认证（签名证书）；2) 采用递归倍增（recursive-doubling）合并：不相交的组独立重算每个中间值，从而把根构建负载分摊到全部参与者并做冗余校验；3) 每节点每周期仅存自身 O(log(n/c)) 的 Merkle 式证明，另加 O(n) 成员花名册；4) 以 Go 原型在 AWS 上部署 8–256 节点、c=4，并注入模拟长尾（straggler）节点进行测量；5) 给出安全性分析：冲突证书可追溯到签名者，证明将签名输入绑定到根，两个冲突根同时最终化需至少 1/3 拜占庭节点（除委员会数指数小的概率外）。

Result: 每成功周期每节点通信开销 O(c log(n/c))，存储每周期 O(log(n/c)) 证明 + O(n) 花名册；AWS 原型（c=4，8–256 节点，含模拟 straggler）周期完成 12–148 ms，证明验证 <1.5 ms，并实测到对数级增长趋势；安全性方面证明冲突证书可定位签名者、证明绑定输入与根、双根最终化需 ≥1/3 拜占庭节点（失败概率随委员会数量指数下降）。

Conclusion: 论文提出的核心系统贡献是：用"委员会认证 + 递归倍增合并 + 组间冗余重算"把承诺根的构建去中心化，在无全网共识的前提下取得 O(c log(n/c)) 的可扩展通信与亚毫秒级证明验证，适用于透明日志/来源登记这类高频、写多读少且需可审计的元数据场景。工程影响在于：为"许可制透明日志/审计登记"提供了一条介于单运营方与区块链之间的第三条路线，可显著降低部署与运营成本；但实际落地仍需社区自行回答成员准入、证明可用性（数据发布与取回）与排除（作恶者剔除）三类治理问题——这也是其作为系统方案的主要未解工程风险（证明可用性依赖带外发布通道，长尾节点对周期时延的影响需进一步刻画）。

Abstract: Commitment services, such as timestamping, transparency logs and provenance registries, let a party prove that it bound itself to a record without publishing the record. Today an operator runs them, or a blockchain replaces the operator at the cost of network-wide agreement on every write. We present Cambium, a permissioned peer-to-peer service in which participants build each cycle's commitment root together, so that no single party decides which inputs the root includes. Fixed-size committees certify inputs and use a recursive-doubling merge, in which disjoint groups recompute every intermediate value, to spread root construction across all participants. A successful cycle costs each node $O(c\log(n/c))$ messages for committee size $c$, and each node stores its own $O(\log(n/c))$ proof per cycle, along with the $O(n)$ membership roster. A Go prototype on AWS with $c=4$, 8-256 nodes and simulated stragglers completes cycles in 12-148 ms and verifies proofs in under 1.5 ms, and demonstrates logarithmic growth. We show that conflicting certificates identify their signers, that a proof binds a signed input to its root, and that two conflicting roots both become final only if at least a third of the nodes are Byzantine, except with probability exponentially small in the number of committees. We also set out the membership, proof-availability and exclusion questions a community deploying Cambium must answer.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

However, GDS retains three key limitations: (1) its control path traverses the kernel for every I/O request, introducing software overhead; (2) its stream-ordered path imposes strict total ordering even when workloads only require batch-level ordering; and (3) its closed-source user-space engine hinders diagnosis and custom scheduling.

In LMCache, uGDS reduces median time-to-first-token (TTFT) by up to 62.7% and raises serving goodput by 2.63x. On AMD, uGDS achieves up to 1.19x hipFile's synchronous-write bandwidth.
