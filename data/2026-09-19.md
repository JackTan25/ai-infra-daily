### [1] [Detecting Soft Errors in Parallel Software with LLM-tuned Instruction Duplication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19531)
*Yafan Huang,Guanpeng Li*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: PaRID 是一个纯编译期、面向多线程并行程序的软错误检测框架，通过并行感知代码变换与 LLM 调优的性能建模，把冗余执行（instruction duplication）的保护开销从平均 162.79% 降至 59.84%，最高加速 5 倍，且保持完整检错能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 并行程序（含串行区与并行区混合）的软错误（soft error，如位翻转导致的静默数据损坏 SDC）检测通常依赖冗余执行/指令复制，带来巨大性能开销。现有方案若要压低开销，往往依赖昂贵的动态 profiling 或运行时信息，难以在编译期一次性完成；同时，串行区与并行区（如 OpenMP parallel region、临界区、reduction）语义不同，冗余复制策略不能一刀切。

Method: 1) 离线表征研究（offline characterization study）提炼出八条可泛化发现，指导复制策略的设计空间；2) 并行感知代码变换（parallel-aware code transformation）：在编译期区分串行/并行区，对循环、reduction、共享/私有变量、同步点采用差异化复制与校验策略，避免在并行区做无效或高代价冗余；3) LLM 调优的性能建模：用大模型辅助构建/校准开销模型，替代在线动态 profiling 来选择复制粒度与位置，从而在编译期完成决策。

Result: 在 NPB 基准上，保护开销由 162.79% 平均降至 59.84%，最高取得 5 倍加速，同时保持与基线相同的完整错误检测有效性（无检错率损失）。

Conclusion: PaRID 表明“编译期决策 + 并行语义感知 + 学习型性能建模”可以在不牺牲检错覆盖的前提下显著降低并行程序软错误保护开销，对可靠性敏感的 HPC/并行负载具有工程落地价值。局限在于评估主要限于 NPB 的数值型并行负载，LLM 建模的泛化性与对新编程模型（GPU、任务并行）的迁移仍需验证。

Abstract: We propose PaRID (PaRallel Instruction Duplication), a software-directed soft error detection framework that requires only compile-time effort for multithreading parallel programs. PaRID addresses two key challenges: supporting parallel programs with mixed serial and parallel regions and minimizing performance overhead without relying on costly dynamic profiling. It combines parallel-aware code transformation with LLM-tuned performance modeling, guided by eight generalizable findings from an offline characterization study, to enable fast soft error detection in parallel applications. Evaluation on NPB benchmarks shows that PaRID reduces protection overhead from 162.79% to 59.84% on average and achieves up to 5x speedup while maintaining full error detection effectiveness.

### [2] [Syndrome Decoding for Silent Data Corruption in Quantized Integer GPU Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19743)
*Pranav Napolean,Vikas Srivastava,Napolean Periathambi*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: SProbe 是一个运行在未修改厂商 GEMM 之后的尾随校验内核：通过 61 位素域上的三评估点随机 Freivalds 门以 ≤2^-141 的漏检概率检测 INT32 累加器的静默数据损坏，再用三素数幂和校验 + Reed-Solomon 译码链（BM/Chien/Forney）逐行定位并恢复最多 4 个碰撞错误的位置与精确幅值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量化 NN 推理在 GPU tensor core 上执行整数矩阵乘，INT32 累加器既无 parity 也无 ECC，瞬态故障会产生"合法但错误"的整数且不触发任何中断（SDC）。现有 checksum/ABFT 方案存在两个结构性缺陷：(1) 判决是二值的，只能报错不能定位元素与幅值；(2) 未加权行/列校验和对称轴抵消型错误天然失明（构造性盲区），TR-ABFT 完全无法检测，加权网格码虽可检测但无法纠正。

Method: 1) 在 vendor GEMM 输出上叠加尾随验证内核，不改动库实现；2) 随机 Freivalds 门：在 61 位素域上取 3 个独立评估点，漏检上界 2^-141；3) 门触发后，对每行用三个素数上的幂和 syndrome，经 Berlekamp-Massey 求错误定位多项式、Chien search 求列位置、Forney 算法求精确幅值，单行最多纠正 4 个碰撞错误；4) 恢复策略二选一：原地修复累加器或重算 GEMM。

Result: H100 上覆盖 7 类故障、4 种矩阵规模，全部注入故障均被检出，包括 TR-ABFT 永不检出的构造模式和加权网格码可检不可纠的模式。门开销：N=16384 时为 cuBLASLt GEMM 时间的 49%，N=65536 时降至 11%。INT8 医疗 LLM 上消除全部观测到的静默损坏，吞吐代价 30%。实测结论：所有配置下重算均快于原地恢复；恢复成本由"诊断"而非"修复"主导；论文还报告了验证 verifier 自身时发现的缺陷。

Conclusion: SProbe 将整数 GEMM 的 ABFT 从"二值告警"推进到"可定位、可度量、可修复"的诊断级容错，且对 vendor kernel 零侵入、开销随 N 增大而摊薄，使其在长序列/大 batch 推理和大规模训练 GEMM 中具备实用价值。重算优于原地修复、诊断主导开销这两点直接指导工程实现：应优先优化 syndrome 计算与 RS 译码路径，并默认以重算作为恢复手段。

Abstract: Quantized neural network inference runs integer matrix multiplications on GPU tensor cores, and the INT32 accumulators inside those cores have neither parity nor ECC. A transient fault in this datapath returns a valid but wrong integer and raises no interrupt. Checksum based Algorithm Based Fault Tolerance (ABFT) can detect such silent data corruptions (SDCs), but its verdict is binary. It cannot identify the corrupted element or its magnitude, and unweighted row and column checksums are blind by construction to errors that cancel on both axes. We present SProbe, a trailing verification kernel that reads the output of an unmodified vendor GEMM. A randomized Freivalds gate with three independent evaluation points in a 61 bit prime field misses a nonzero error with probability at most $2^{-141}$. When the gate fires, per row power sum syndromes over three primes are decoded with the Reed Solomon chain of Berlekamp Massey, Chien search, and Forney, recovering the column and exact magnitude of up to four colliding errors per row. SProbe then repairs the accumulator in place or recomputes the GEMM. On an NVIDIA H100, SProbe detects every injected fault across seven fault classes and four matrix sizes, including constructed patterns that TR-ABFT never detects and patterns that a weighted grid code detects but cannot correct. The gate costs 49% of the cuBLASLt GEMM time at N=16384 and 11% at N=65536. In an INT8 medical LLM, protection eliminates all observed silent corruptions at a 30% throughput cost. Our measurements also show that recomputation is faster than in place recovery in every configuration we tested, that diagnosis rather than repair dominates recovery cost, and we report the defects we found while validating the verifier itself.

</details>

### [4] [Sketching the Error, Not the Product: Post Hoc Fault Recovery for Half Precision GPU Matrix Multiplication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19758)
*Pranav Napolean,Vikas Srivastava,Napolean Periathambi*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: FP-Sketch 是一个运行在未修改 tensor core GEMM 之后的校验器：用 sum sketch 检测每次调用的静默数据损坏（SDC），再用哈希一阶矩 sketch + 独立重算定位多个损坏条目，无假阳性，并为每个故障输出坐标与幅度以支持集群级诊断。关键发现是浮点下限制定位精度的是 sketch 噪声而非桶碰撞，噪声常数依赖 BLAS 与操作数格式，桶数需按 n^2.57 增长；按实测噪声而非 n 的幂次拟合来定桶数，可把八种 transformer 形状的恢复率从 0.402 提到 1.000。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 缺陷加速器导致的静默数据损坏（SDC）会中断大规模训练，但现有部署的缓解措施只作用于整节点粒度。针对单次 GEMM 的 ABFT 必须融合进 kernel 或编码操作数，且每个校验和最多只能定位一个错误，无法满足细粒度、多错误定位与故障诊断的需求。

Method: FP-Sketch 以"后置校验器"形态工作，GEMM 本身不做任何修改，操作数为半精度、累加与输出为 FP32。检测阶段用 sum sketch 覆盖每次调用；定位阶段用哈希一阶矩 sketch 找出候选损坏条目，再通过独立重算确认，从而在构造上保证无假阳性，并为每个故障给出坐标与幅度。作者分析了浮点下噪声（而非桶碰撞）是定位精度的限制因素，测量噪声常数对 BLAS 与操作数格式的依赖，并推导方形乘积下桶数需按 n^2.57 增长。实现上按实测噪声定桶数，并支持运行时测量噪声以自适应 kernel 与模型。评估使用 NVBit 做指令级注入，并与输出侧注入对比。

Result: 按实测噪声（而非拟合的 n 幂次）确定桶数，使八个 transformer 形状的恢复率从 0.402 提升到 1.000；运行时测量噪声可自适应 kernel 与模型。NVBit 指令级注入显示，活跃累加器上的翻转往往只有典型条目的 2%~9%，这是输出侧注入无法产生的故障分布：输出侧注入可恢复全部故障，而同一引擎在 NVBit 下按典型幅度定尺寸只恢复 0.550，按实测幅度定尺寸恢复 1.000。在 Llama-2-7B 上，保护 MLP down projection 消除了 2048 次 bit flip 造成困惑度损害的 99.4%（BF16）与 99.9%（FP16）。干净路径探针开销 0.78~3.06 ms，对应 GEMM 为 0.35~12.47 ms。

Conclusion: FP-Sketch 证明可在不改动 tensor core GEMM 的前提下实现调用粒度的 SDC 检测与多错误定位，并把浮点噪声而非哈希碰撞确立为定位精度的核心约束；其按实测噪声自适应 sizing 的策略与故障坐标/幅度输出，使 SDC 缓解从整节点粒度下沉到 kernel 调用与具体坐标，便于集群级故障诊断。代价是探针开销可能与小 GEMM 同量级，需要在覆盖率与运行时预算间权衡。

Abstract: Silent data corruption (SDC) from defective accelerators now interrupts large scale training, yet deployed mitigations act on whole nodes. Algorithm based fault tolerance (ABFT) for a single GEMM has to be fused into the kernel or encode the operands, and it localizes at most one error per checksum. We present FP-Sketch, a verifier that runs after an unmodified tensor core GEMM whose half precision operands are accumulated and delivered at FP32. A sum sketch detects corruption on every call. Hashed first moment sketches, confirmed by independent recomputation, then localize several corrupted entries with no false positives by construction, and each fault yields a coordinate and a magnitude for fleet diagnosis. In floating point, sketch noise rather than bucket collisions limits localization. We measure that noise and find that its constant depends on the BLAS and the operand format and that the bucket count must grow as $n^{2.57}$ for a square product. Sizing the bucket count by measured noise rather than by a fitted power of $n$ raises recovery on eight transformer shapes from 0.402 to 1.000, and measuring the noise at run time adapts the bucket count to the kernel and the model. Instruction level injection with NVBit shows that upsets in a live accumulator are often only 2 to 9% of a typical entry, a population that output side injection cannot produce. Output side injection recovers every fault, while under NVBit the same engine sized for faults of typical magnitude recovers 0.550, and sizing for the measured magnitudes restores 1.000. On Llama-2-7B, guarding the MLP down projections removes 99.4% (BF16) and 99.9% (FP16) of the perplexity damage caused by 2048 bit flips, and the clean path probe costs 0.78 to 3.06 ms against GEMMs of 0.35 to 12.47 ms.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Foundation models (FMs) are a crucial example, requiring months-long training on thousands of cutting-edge GPUs. Sharded data parallelism (DP) is the dominant strategy to accelerate such computations by splitting data and models across multiple GPUs.
