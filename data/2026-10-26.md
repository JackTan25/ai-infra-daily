### [1] [KVStream: Adaptive Parallel Storage Management for KV Caching in Large-Scale LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31827)
*Avinash Maurya,Jaiaid Mobin,Jie Ye,M. Mustafa Rafique,Franck Cappello,Bogdan Nicolae*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: KVStream 将长上下文前缀 KV 缓存检索建模为跨 GPU、主机内存、节点本地 NVMe 与并行/云文件系统的聚合带宽调度问题，通过按步批量化读、延迟写、多层复制与"谁空闲谁抢占下一未读块"的自适应路由，在无需预测与调参的前提下逼近聚合带宽上界，并作为 LMCache 后端在 128 GPU 规模上取得显著 TTFT 与吞吐收益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Agentic、代码与文档类长上下文负载存在大量前缀复用，其 KV 缓存规模已超出 GPU 与主机内存容量，使前缀检索的存储 I/O 进入推理步的关键路径。然而现有系统通常把检索压力集中在单节点 NVMe 上，而已挂载的并行文件系统/云文件存储几乎不承载推理流量，形成明显的带宽闲置与瓶颈并存。此外，各层带宽会漂移与波动，静态的分层切分策略会随带宽漂移偏离最优，误差随波动增大。

Method: 1) 将前缀检索形式化为跨存储层的聚合带宽调度问题，给出性能模型并论证自适应路由可达到聚合带宽上界，而任意静态切分在带宽漂移/抖动下存在系统性差距。2) KVStream 引擎：将一个推理步内的读请求批量化统一提交；把写（KV 落盘/复制）推迟到前向计算之后，避免与关键路径读竞争带宽；每个 chunk 在各层均保留副本以支持多源并行读取；采用无状态的"空闲层自适应认领下一未读 chunk"路由，无需带宽预测或调参；TTFT 收益随负载上升（快速检索排空队列），在主机内存命中或到达顺序利于复用时不显现。3) 作为 LMCache 的存储后端集成。

Result: 在 4 个模型、6 个数据集、最多 128 GPU 上评测：检索性能位于理论上界 5% 以内；离线吞吐比 LMCache 最高提升 1.49$\times$；突发到达场景下平均 TTFT 最多降低 6.1$\times$。

Conclusion: 该工作指出长上下文 KV 复用的瓶颈已从显存容量转向多层存储的聚合带宽利用率，并证明简单的自适应认领式路由可在免预测、免调参条件下逼近上界，兼具可移植性与工程实用性；对 KV 缓存分层（GPU/DRAM/NVMe/分布式文件系统）的系统设计与 LMCache 类前缀缓存中间件的部署具有直接参考价值。

Abstract: Long-context LLM serving for agentic, coding, and document workloads reuses prefixes whose key-value (KV) cache outgrows GPU and host memory, putting storage I/O on the critical path of the inference step for retrieving prefixes. A single node-local NVMe absorbs all retrievals, while the parallel file system or cloud file store that nodes already mount carries almost no inference traffic. We cast prefix retrieval as aggregate-bandwidth scheduling across these tiers and present KVStream, a KV cache engine that batches a step's reads, defers writes behind the forward pass, replicates each chunk on every tier, and lets each tier adaptively claim the next unread chunks once it is free. Our performance model shows this routing attains the aggregate-bandwidth bound without prediction or tuning, whereas any static split misses it by a margin that grows as per-tier bandwidths drift or fluctuate. It also predicts that the time-to-first-token (TTFT) gain grows with load, as faster retrieval drains the queue, and disappears when host memory or arrival order keeps reuse in cache. Plugged into LMCache and evaluated on four models, six datasets, and up to 128 GPUs, KVStream retrieves within 5% of the bound, delivers up to 1.49$\times$ higher offline throughput than LMCache, and lowers mean TTFT by up to 6.1$\times$ under bursty arrivals.

Foundation models workflows and automated pipelines have made job submissions bursty and concentrated among a few heavy users, so occasional users absorb queueing delays created by frequent ones, with no indication of how long their jobs will wait. HyDRA achieves $2\times$ the project-level Jain's fairness of the best baseline, starts 47\% of jobs by their deadlines compared with at most 36\% for the baselines even for largest workloads, and lowers the 95th-\%ile bounded slowdown by 4.2$\times$ relative to the next best baseline, at a moderate cost in utilization.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
