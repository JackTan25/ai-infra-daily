### [1] [Runway Characters: Serving a 57B Video Diffusion Transformer for Real-Time Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05642)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 交互式视频角色要求"生成速度≥播放速度"，且必须 batch=1 串行低延迟，不能靠吞吐换延迟。分析显示每 chunk 的 240 次 attention 调用都在读 5788 槽 KV 缓存，而其中仅 757 token 是新的，约 29% 的原始 step 时间消耗在每次调用都会重复的"整缓存不变工作"上（RoPE 应用、FP8 量化、缓存读放大）。因此核心动机是消除这部分冗余计算并压缩 TP 通信与解码开销。

Method: 1) 写入时计算（write-time work）：环形 KV 缓存管理；RoPE 与 FP8 量化在每 token 写入时只做一次而非每次 attention 调用重做；位置回绕/重置时用 delta rotation 而非重算整缓存。2) 通信-计算融合：将 tensor-parallel all-reduce 融合进 FP8 GEMM，采用 in-switch（交换机内/GEMM 内部）归约，降低 all-reduce 暴露延迟与激活往返。3) 资源解耦：为 latent→像素的解码器（VAE decode）分配独立 GPU，避免其与扩散主干争用同一卡的算力/带宽。4) 语音 agent 在环的流式流水线，配少量 chunk 缓冲以对齐 166.7ms/4帧的硬性节拍。

Result: 不含融合 kernel：240.9ms → 159.3ms（1.51×，持续 24.5fps），唇同步评分无可检测退化。含融合 kernel：147.4ms（1.63×），达到 FP8 峰值的 43%（该 kernel 曾在生产回滚一次，后重新启用）。生产 7 周：在持续流式 ≥1s 的会话中，99.5% 的会话平均帧率 >23.5fps；端到端流水线占 2.1s 中位对话轮次的 0.49s。对比：两个开源实时视频系统在相同 GPU、同样 4 次去噪下，要么无法实时要么额外增加 >1s 延迟（其中一项为估算），且分辨率为本系统的 1/3~1/2。

Conclusion: 该工作表明：batch=1 的低延迟交互式视频生成中，瓶颈不仅是算力，更是"每步重复的整缓存工作 + TP 通信暴露 + 解码器抢占"这类结构性开销；把不变量前移到写入时、把通信融进 GEMM、把解码剥离到独立设备，可在不重训模型、不损失质量的前提下取得 1.5~1.6× 端到端收益，并把大模型（57B）交互式视频推进到生产可用的实时区间。工程含义：实时多模态服务的优化重心应向状态生命周期（KV 缓存的写时预处理）与通信-计算同构融合倾斜，而非仅做 kernel 级微调。

Abstract: Conversational video characters must generate video as fast as it plays, for one user at a time: at 24 fps, a sustained 166.7 ms per four-frame chunk at batch size one, with only a few chunks of buffering. We describe the system behind Runway Characters, which serves a $\approx$57B-parameter autoregressive video diffusion transformer (four denoising passes per chunk) at 704$\times$1088 and 24 fps on four H100 GPUs with a voice agent in the loop. Each chunk takes 240 attention calls, each reading a 5,788-slot KV cache in which only 757 tokens are new, and 29% of the original step was whole-cache work repeated in every call. We move that work to write time (a ring cache, RoPE and FP8 quantization applied once per write, and a delta rotation when positions reset), fuse the tensor-parallel all-reduce into the FP8 GEMM with in-switch reduction, and give the decoder that turns latents into video frames its own GPU. Without the fused kernel, our optimizations cut the diffusion step from 240.9 to 159.3 ms (1.51$\times$, 24.5 fps sustained) with no detected degradation in lip-sync scores; the fused kernel, rolled back once in production and since re-enabled, reaches 147.4 ms (1.63$\times$, 43% of FP8 peak). In seven weeks of production the worker averaged above 23.5 fps in 99.5% of sessions that streamed for at least a second, and our pipeline takes 0.49 s of a 2.1 s median conversational turn. On the same GPUs, the two open-source real-time video systems we benchmarked, at four denoising passes, miss real time or add over a second of latency (one by estimate), at a third to half of our resolution.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: EE-RL 是一个在线控制回路：1) 按阶段分离的频率决策——生成阶段与训练阶段各维护一组候选 SM/内存频率；2) 以「同一 token 负载下默认 governor 的预测表现」作为比较基线，避免昂贵的长时实测；3) 设定吞吐阈值作为约束，在满足约束的候选中选能效最优（能量/有效 token 最小）者；4) 运行期监控吞吐与能耗，检测负载变化并重新调整频率，从而把离线扫描发现的静态余量转为在线收益。评估覆盖 H100 与 GB200，并同时考察同步与异步 RL 执行模式。

Conclusion: 论文的核心 systems 贡献是把「阶段感知的 GPU 频率管理」从离线剖析推进到在线控制器，并把能效度量对齐到 RL 训练的经济学单位（每有效训练 token 的能量），而非整卡利用率或平均功耗等粗粒度指标。工程影响：这类控制可用于 RL 后训练集群的功耗封顶与成本优化，并为调度器提供「阶段/频率/吞吐」三维权衡接口，与异步 rollout、分离式生成-训练部署天然契合。局限在于收益依赖阶段计算特性差异、仍以部分吞吐换取能效、且控制回路需与集群级功率上限和 SLO 协同，跨模型规模与异构加速器的泛化性有待验证。

Abstract: Reinforcement learning for language models alternates between generating responses and training the policy. These stages place different demands on GPUs, creating opportunities to reduce clock frequency with little loss in throughput. Selecting frequencies online is difficult because response lengths change and frequency trials themselves consume time and energy. We present EE-RL (energy-efficient RL), an online controller that selects separate frequencies for generation and training during RL execution. It compares candidate settings with a prediction of the default GPU governor on the same token workload, and keeps the most energy-efficient setting that meets a throughput threshold. The controller then monitors throughput and energy and adjusts frequencies as the workload changes. Fixed-frequency sweeps on H100 and GB200 show GPU energy savings of up to 28.2% per valid training token across synchronous and asynchronous execution, while largely preserving throughput. EE-RL turns this headroom into online savings, reducing GPU energy per valid training token by up to 16.9% at a modest throughput cost.
