Motivation: 地面5G/6G部署集中在高密度区域，无法覆盖偏远地区和应急场景，存在数字鸿沟。LEO卫星网络可弥补这一缺口，但天地网络在互操作、频谱、管控、服务连续性等方面面临集成难题，需要系统性的体系架构研究与方案论证。

Method: 采用"集成+联邦"双轨思路：集成解决TN与NTN的深度协同问题；联邦通过协议协同、统一卫星终端与共享资源管理实现异构卫星系统的合作。同时考察Open-RAN架构在T-NTN中的落地适配，包括mega-LEO路由、边缘智能与可持续性能效方案，并从零信任、安全路由、可信边缘智能、PQC与QKD维度构建安全体系。

Result: 通过对比分析证明：联邦式T-NTN（fed-NTN）相比非联邦LEO架构在延迟性能和连接鲁棒性上均有显著提升。论文系统性地梳理了T-NTN集成全栈开放性问题及候选技术路径。

Conclusion: 天地一体化网络需要"集成+联邦"互补推进，LEO是支撑全球连续覆盖的关键。Open-RAN为T-NTN提供开放可编程的架构基础，但需同步解决路由、边缘智能与能效挑战；安全层面需转向零信任+后量子密码+QKD的组合防御范式，方能在异构多运营商协同场景下保障系统韧性。

Abstract: This study focuses on future Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN) integrated with Terrestrial Networks (TN) for future 5G/6G systems. NTN envisions a 3D architecture, where Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks will play a key role in bridging the digital divide, complementing the gradual terrestrial 5G/6G rollout concentrated in high-density and high-traffic areas, by ensuring service continuity across broad geographic regions and providing coverage in case of emergencies or in remote areas. In this context, we address networking issues for the integration and federation of Terrestrial and Non-Terrestrial Network (T-NTN) in line with the IMT-2030 vision, focusing on interoperability, spectrum coexistence, unified control and management, and service continuity. Federation is a complementary approach to integration that enables distinct satellite systems to cooperate through agreements, potentially unified satellite terminals, and common resource management. We show that system federation significantly enhances both latency performance and connectivity robustness compared with non-federated LEO architectures. This paper also investigates the challenges and possible solutions for adopting the Open-RAN architecture for T-NTN, including routing options for mega-LEO systems, edge intelligence, and energy efficiency as critical elements for sustainability. Finally, we address the security, privacy, and resilience aspects of federated T-NTN architectures with emphasis on zero-trust, secure routing, trustworthy edge intelligence, Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), and Quantum Key Distribution (QKD).
