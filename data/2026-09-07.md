### [1] [Performance Study of Serverless Workloads in Confidential Virtual Machines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04478)
*Rikesh Niroula,Jianchen Shan,Xiaoning Ding*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 本文对机密虚拟机上运行的 serverless 工作负载进行实证研究，系统性地覆盖了内存效率和冷/热启动下的运行时开销。研究发现 CVM 因加密内存禁用跨 VM 页去重并降低内存可回收性而带来显著内存开销，限制热容器容量；热启动的运行时开销依赖工作负载，频繁 VMEXIT（尤其空闲转换）的工作负载严重变慢，且被 vCPU 与内存绑定分配的常见实践进一步放大。针对冷启动，研究提出在同一 CVM 中整合相同函数的多个容器，并选择性地放宽某些隔离机制可显著降低启动开销。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Across four compound AI workflows, MAP achieves Spearman correlation up to 0.947 while reducing profiling cost by up to 2.6x relative to exhaustive end-to-end profiling. Guided by MAP, the Atlas optimizer selects execution plans within 0.03 of oracle accuracy while reducing deployment cost by up to 42% through heterogeneous placement.
