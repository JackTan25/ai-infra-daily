### [1] [Cyclotron: A Recurrence Language for Expressing Interprocessor Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20981)
*Shiv Sundram,Akhilesh Balasingam,Nathan Zhang,Kunle Olukotun,Fredrik Kjolstad*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: Cyclotron 是一个编译框架：用户用"递推方程(recurrence equations)"在逻辑迭代空间上描述流式数据流算法及其点间通信，编译器根据用户选择的 space–time 映射自动生成每进程的 send/recv/compute 程序，实现算法与硬件映射的解耦。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分布式线性代数库（ScaLAPACK/SLATE/cuSolverMp）把算法结构与通信方案、进程网格绑定，用户难以表达非标准通信模式（脉动近邻交换、one-to-all 广播、双向分裂环、非对称分段广播）；改变并行划分或时空映射往往要重写整个程序。

Method: 1) 语言层：以迭代空间上的递推方程表示数据流，数据可"驻留"在迭代空间内部点上，点间可直接通信，通信模式由用户在递推中显式声明；2) 映射层：用户仅指定哪些维度跨进程（space）、哪些维度沿时间执行（time），不重写程序；3) 编译层：依据用户声明的跨迭代空间数据依赖，编译器在所选 space–time 映射下推导并安全放置通信与计算算子，生成每处理器的 send/recv/compute 程序（含分布拓扑的构造）。

Result: 在最多 16 个 CPU 节点与 16 块 GPU 上评测分布式矩阵乘、TRSM、Cholesky 及融合 Cholesky–TRSM，与 ScaLAPACK、SLATE、cuSolverMp 对比：性能具竞争力且常优于手工优化库；对 Cholesky 而言，最优通信策略随问题规模和体系结构变化。

Conclusion: 将"算法/通信描述"与"硬件映射"分离，并由编译器完成通信放置，是一条可行且高效的路线；由于最优通信策略随规模与架构迁移，可编程、可搜索映射空间（而非固定库内策略）成为获取性能的关键，对分布式算子库与编译器协同设计有直接参考价值。

Abstract: We present Cyclotron, a compiler that expresses streaming dataflow algorithms using recurrence equations, which are then compiled to distributed topologies of interlinked processors. Our compiler lowers a language of recurrences over logical iteration spaces into per-processor programs of sends, receives, and computations. In these recurrences, data accesses are not limited to external accesses to memory; data can live separately on the iteration space itself, allowing internal points to communicate values directly with other points. Users specify this communication directly in the recurrence language, which allows them to express various communication patterns such as a nearest-neighbor systolic exchange, a one-to-all broadcast, a bidirectional split ring, or an asymmetric and piecewise broadcast pattern, which are often not supported by standard distributed linear algebra libraries. These recurrences describe the algorithm and communication independently of how they map onto the hardware. A programmer then chooses which dimensions execute across processors and which execute over time, without rewriting the entire program. Users only specify data dependencies across the iteration space, and the compiler safely generates a program, per process, with correct placement of both communication and computational operators according to the chosen space--time mapping. We evaluate distributed matrix multiplication, TRSM, Cholesky, and fused Cholesky--TRSM on up to 16 CPU nodes and 16 GPUs, comparing against ScaLAPACK, SLATE, and cuSolverMp. Cyclotron is competitive with and often outperforms these hand-optimized libraries; for Cholesky, the best communication strategy changes with problem size and architecture.

### [3] [Coordination-Free Workload Regularization for Variable-Cost Distributed Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21162)
*Tao Huang,Chen Zhu,Antonio Alonso,Daming Li,Adarsh Goura,Jaideep Ray,Yitong Zhou*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 本文提出 Batch Cost Lookahead (BCL)，一种无需跨 rank 协调的本地数据加载策略：在每个 rank 内缓冲有界窗口，并按估计代价重排为固定基数（cardinality）的 pack，使得各 rank 的每步批次代价更均衡，从而在同步数据并行训练中减少由变长样本引起的等待与空转。仿真中使每 rank 批次代价方差最多降低 82.8%；在 8×H200 上 13 个 NeMo-RL SFT 语料上相对 pooled MFFD 提速 1.050–1.167×；内部序列负载的编译实测在 1–4 节点上相对 packed 执行提速 1.197–1.343×，并给出 p99 上界的内存可行性边界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 同步数据并行训练通常给每个 rank 分配相同数量的记录（record-count 契约），但变长/变代价模型下记录代价可相差数个数量级。由于每步都要等待最慢 rank 的本地 batch（straggler），输入长度方差直接转化为 step 级空转和 GPU 利用率损失。已有的 cross-rank 全局 pool/打包方案需要元数据交换或运行时改动，部署成本高。

Abstract: Synchronous data-parallel training usually assigns every rank the same number of records, even when record costs vary by orders of magnitude. For variable-length models, this converts input variation into idle time because each step waits for its most expensive rank-local batch. BCL preserves the trainer's record-count contract and requires neither cross-rank metadata exchange nor runtime modification. In simulation over public workload distributions, enabling BCL reduces per-rank estimated batch-cost variability by up to 82.8%. Across 13 NeMo-RL SFT corpora on eight H200 GPUs, rank-local BCL runs 1.050 to 1.167 times faster than pooled MFFD. Compiled H200 measurements on an internal sequence workload show speedups of 1.197 to 1.343 times over packed execution from one to four nodes. We also identify a memory-feasibility boundary: a p99 cap for high-variance or OOM-prone data bounds outliers before BCL balances the rest. These results show that substantial variable-cost imbalance can be removed at the data-loader boundary, while clarifying when data preparation or broader coordination is still necessary.

### [5] [Exact Makespan Bounds and the GPipe Homogeneity Bias for Heterogeneous LLM Inference Pipelines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21815)
*Zhu Wenbo*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 论文对异构 GPU 集群上 LLM 微批流水线（micro-batch pipeline）的 makespan 给出了精确的 critical-path 刻画，量化了经典同构 GPipe 公式的误差，并给出三类通信模型下的闭式解：无通信纯前向、异步通信（NCCL 类 serving）、阻塞通信。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 1) 损伤状态推断：由稀疏局部上报反推全局失效状态，且不上传位置敏感坐标（隐私/安全约束下的可观测性补全）。2) AdDiff-GT：物理启发的图Transformer，将平流-扩散（advection-diffusion）动力学嵌入节点-超节点混合图；簇内用局部消息传递建模结构关系，簇间用全局注意力建模交互。3) 加权复合损失：连通性恢复+机动代价+运动一致性+簇间协同+空间保持的联合优化。4) 元学习：跨损伤模式与集群规模学习可迁移初始化，支持GCS在线快速自适应与机载分布式推理。5) 仿真覆盖8000个分区场景（含5000大规模）。

Result: 在8000个分区场景（其中5000个为大规模）上，AdDiff-GT在拓扑恢复与空间保持两方面优于6个代表性基线；元学习显著降低在线规划开销，代价是恢复步数略有增加，即在先验迁移与在线自适应之间取得折中。

Conclusion: 该工作把"不可观测性下的状态推断"与"物理先验引导的图学习式拓扑重构"结合，为对抗场景下集群自愈提供了可在线部署的框架；元学习的引入表明跨规模/跨故障模式的可迁移初始化可在几乎不损失恢复质量的前提下换取明显的规划时延收益。不过评估完全基于仿真，缺少真实链路模型、通信开销与端到端时延的量化，建议作为后续工程验证重点。

Abstract: This paper investigates the resilience and self-healing of unmanned aerial vehicle swarm communication networks under severe network partitioning in complex and adversarial environments. To address limited global observability after damage, we develop a damage-state inference mechanism that enables the ground control station (GCS) to infer global failures from sparse local reports without transmitting location-sensitive coordinates. To recover multi-cluster partitioned topologies, we then propose AdDiff-GT, a physics-inspired graph Transformer that incorporates advection-diffusion dynamics into a node-supernode hybrid graph. Local message propagation captures intra-cluster structural relationships, while global attention models inter-cluster interactions. A weighted composite loss jointly considers connectivity restoration, maneuvering cost, motion consistency, inter-cluster coordination, and spatial preservation during model training. We further develop a meta-learning strategy for transferable initialization across damage patterns and swarm scales, enabling efficient online GCS adaptation and distributed airborne inference. Simulations on 8,000 partitioned scenarios, including 5,000 large-scale cases, show that AdDiff-GT achieves effective topology recovery and spatial preservation over six representative baselines. Meta-learning reduces online planning overhead with a minor increase in recovery steps, balancing prior knowledge transfer and online adaptation.
