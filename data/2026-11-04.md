### [1] [INSIGHT-HPC: An Interpretable System Intelligence for Global HPC Telemetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03875)
*Rupak Kadel,Jie Li,Yong Chen*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: INSIGHT-HPC 是一个面向生产级 HPC 集群的多平面异常检测架构，将统计偏差、负载感知（job scheduler 上下文）与物理一致性（功耗/热力学/BMC 传感器关系）三类证据融合为可被运维人员解读的"故障事件（fault episode）"，在大规模异构 telemetry 上显著优于统计基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代 HPC 集群混部多代 CPU/GPU，BMC（带外管理）、作业调度器和机房 PDU 产生高维遥测流；异常会造成能耗浪费与昂贵节点故障。现有方法主要依赖统计偏差检测，且需要生产中无法获得的强标签，同时告警缺乏运维上下文，导致大量"由负载波动解释的正常行为"被误报、告警不可解释、无法落地闭环处理。

Method: 多平面（multi-plane）检测架构：①统计平面——对分钟级高维时序做统计偏差检测；②负载感知平面——引入 job scheduler 信息，把可被作业负载解释的行为从基础设施异常中剥离；③物理引导平面——利用 PDU/BMC 传感器间的物理一致性约束（功耗-利用率-温度等关系）判别真实硬件/设施异常。上游做异构大规模遥测的统一接入与融合，下游把噪声分钟级观测聚类、去重为"故障事件"episode，并生成面向运维的解释性告警摘要（episode-level fusion + explanation）。评测使用生产遥测 + 弱运维标签 + 受控故障注入。

Existing approaches often rely primarily on statistical deviations and require stronger labels than actual production environments can provide. In addition, these detectors have a limited operational context for interpreting alerts. INSIGHT-HPC integrates heterogeneous, large-scale telemetry streams and transforms noisy minute-resolution observations into de-duplicated fault episodes with operator-facing explanations. This design allows the system to distinguish workload-explained behavior from genuine infrastructure anomalies while producing interpretable alert summaries for operators. On production HPC telemetry with weak operational labels and controlled HPC-relevant fault injections, INSIGHT-HPC achieves a stronger precision-recall balance than all evaluated baselines. The system achieves a precision of 0.913, a recall of 0.817, an F1 score of 0.862, and an AUROC of 0.896. These results demonstrate the value of combining workload context, physical consistency, and explainable episode-level fusion.

### [2] [EpiLoRA: Fusing Many LoRA Adapters into Low-Precision GEMMs for Serving and RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03899)
*Hieu Tran,Hong Yu*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: EpiLoRA 是 vLLM 的一个插件，利用 "LoRA 的 down-projection 只在低精度 GEMM 的 epilogue 阶段才需要" 这一关键观察，将 down-projection 作为前置独立 kernel 与 FP8/INT4 GEMM 级联（GEMM 在流式加载权重期间完成 down-proj），并在 epilogue 中融合各租户的 up-projection，从而消除多租户 LoRA 服务中独立小 kernel 的延迟开销。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多租户 serving 场景下，单份 base model + 多份 LoRA adapter 是主流形态。当 base model 量化到 FP8/INT4 后，base GEMM 成本持续下降，但 adapter 仍以独立的小 kernel 形式在 GEMM 旁边执行（down-proj + up-proj），这些 kernel 的启动与访存延迟在每步 decoding 中的占比越来越大，成为新的瓶颈。同时，RL 训练侧也存在 base 权重低精度化后需额外保留 16-bit 副本的显存问题。

Method: 核心观察：GEMM 计算主输出时并不需要 down-projection 结果，只有到 epilogue（写回输出、做加法/缩放的最后阶段）才需要它。因此：(1) 先发射 down-projection kernel；(2) 将低精度 GEMM 作为 dependent kernel 发射，在其流式加载量化权重的时间窗口中与 down-proj 形成流水重叠；(3) 在 GEMM 的 epilogue 中直接按租户索引累加对应 up-projection，实现 kernel 融合，避免中间张量物化与额外 launch。工程上以 vLLM 插件形式实现，无需改动引擎主体。训练侧：LoRA RL trainer 直接在 engine 的 FP8 权重上计算，不保留 16-bit 副本；推理引擎将 activation 保持在 16-bit 以缓解训推数值不一致。

Result: NVIDIA Hopper 上：单 adapter 场景下每 token 解码时间比 vLLM 最快配置低最多 24%，并快于 SGLang 与 TensorRT-LLM；多租户场景下，在多数测试的 GPU–model 组合上 adapter kernel 吞吐优于 vLLM。训练侧，27B 模型可在单张 48 GB GPU 上完成 LoRA RL 训练；engine 保持 16-bit activation 可消除生成数据模型与训练模型之间的大部分数值失配。

Conclusion: EpiLoRA 表明，低精度 base + LoRA 的服务优化空间不在 GEMM 本身，而在于把 adapter 的算子语义重新映射到 GEMM 的执行阶段（epilogue）以消除小 kernel 的串行延迟；该思路对 vLLM 这类分页注意力/continuous batching 引擎是可插拔的，且能自然延伸到"量化 base 上直接做 RL 训练"的场景，形成推理与训练共用同一份低精度权重的统一栈。

Abstract: Low-rank adapters (LoRA) let one base model serve many fine-tuned variants: multi-tenant engines keep a single copy of the base and batch the requests of many tenants, each with its own adapter. As base models move to 8-bit (FP8) and 4-bit formats, their general matrix multiplications (GEMMs) become cheaper, but the adapters do not: engines run them as separate small GPU kernels beside the GEMM, whose latency takes a growing share of every decoding step. An adapter projects its input down to a low rank and back up, and our key observation is that the GEMM needs the down-projection only in its epilogue, the final stage in which it writes its output. EpiLoRA, a plugin for the vLLM engine, therefore launches the down-projection first and the low-precision GEMM as a dependent kernel that streams its weights meanwhile and adds every tenant's up-projection in its epilogue. On NVIDIA Hopper GPUs, EpiLoRA decodes for a single adapter with up to 24% lower time per token than vLLM's fastest configuration, and faster than SGLang and TensorRT-LLM; with many tenants, its adapter kernels deliver more throughput than vLLM's on most GPU-model pairs we tested. The same low-precision base also trains: a LoRA reinforcement-learning trainer that computes on the engine's FP8 weights needs no 16-bit copy, so a 27B model trains on one 48 GB GPU, and an engine that then keeps activations in 16 bits removes most of the remaining mismatch between the model generating training data and the one trained.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

In fused operators such as DeepGEMM's Mega MoE, dispatch and computation overlap only by sharing every SM: dispatch warps compete with TMA/MMA and epilogue warps for registers, shared memory, and issue slots; every phase shares one CTA resource budget, synchronization spans the whole SM array, and every replay ends with a workspace-clean pass and a cross-rank barrier. Each role gets its own kernel, resource budget, and synchronization domain, so dispatch and GEMM tune independently. On 8 H20 GPUs GC-MoE accelerates DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash decode by 1.188x on average on both the routed and shared-expert suites (peaks 1.296x and 1.353x); on 8 B300 GPUs it accelerates GLM-5.2 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash by 1.106x (1.123x with fused shared experts, peak 1.209x), beating Mega MoE at all 54 measured points at matched accuracy. GC-MoE reserves only 20 of 148 SMs on B300 for dispatch.
