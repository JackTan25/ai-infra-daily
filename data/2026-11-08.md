### [1] [NUMA-Torch: Compiler-Driven NUMA-Aware LLM Prefill on Multi-Socket CPUs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07105)
*Thisara Rathnayaka Weerakoon,Ishan Dewpura,Niman Ransindu,Shavin Anjitha,Tharindu R. Patabandi,Sanath Jayasena*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: 在多路（multi-socket）CPU 上运行大型 Transformer LLM 的 prefill 阶段时，性能瓶颈是可用的 DRAM 带宽而非浮点峰值；而 TorchInductor 默认不感知 NUMA 拓扑，权重跨 socket 分布导致约大量访存跨越互联，浪费有效带宽。作者在编译器层实现 NUMA-aware TorchInductor，把每个 NUMA socket 当作独立 tensor-parallel 分片（NUMA-TP），配合 CPU affinity 使线程池与内存分配严格本地化，从而消除跨 socket 流量。在 GPT-2 117M/345M/762M、BERT Small/Large、Gemma 3 270M 上，双路 Haswell 最高 2.63x、双路 Cascade Lake 1.87x、四路 Haswell-EX 2.27x 的 prefill 加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当模型参数无法驻留片上缓存时，多路 CPU 上的 prefill 受限于可持续 DRAM 带宽，而非 FLOPS。PyTorch 默认编译路径（TorchInductor）在分配权重时不考虑 NUMA 拓扑，忽略内存 bank 与计算核心的亲和性，使相当比例的权重视访存穿越 socket 间互联（UPI/QPI），每 socket 的有效带宽被显著削减。已有工作多在运行时/库层（如 NUMA-aware 分配器、线程绑定）打补丁，缺少在编译器中同时打通“权重分片 + 计算划分 + 线程/内存亲和”的统一方案，无法从图层面重塑数据布局与并行划分。

Method: 提出 NUMA-aware Tensor Parallelism（NUMA-TP），作为编译器级实现嵌入 TorchInductor：(1) 把每个 NUMA socket 视为独立的 tensor-parallel 分区，在图中对权重做透明分片（按 socket 数切分线性层/注意力权重），并在需要时用 all-reduce 或局部归约拼接激活；(2) 调度计算 kernel 时对线程池与内存分配施加 CPU affinity，使各 socket 的线程池只访问本地内存、分配器只在本地 node 上分配，从而严格保证数据局部性；(3) 对 MHA/MLP（GPT-2、BERT）与 MQA + GeGLU-gated MLP（Gemma 3 270M）等不同结构验证通用性。评估平台：双路 Haswell、双路 Cascade Lake、四路 Haswell-EX。

Result: 在保持数值正确的前提下，通过强制数据局部化显著抑制跨 socket 流量，prefill 加速最高达：双路 Haswell 2.63x、双路 Cascade Lake 1.87x、四路 Haswell-EX 2.27x。加速幅度与 socket 数、互联代际（Cascade Lake 的 UPI 带宽更高，故收益较小）以及模型规模相关；模型越大、访存受限越严重，收益越明显。

Conclusion: 表明“NUMA 拓扑感知”应作为编译器的一等公民：把 NUMA 域映射为 tensor-parallel 维度、并将线程与内存绑定编译进 kernel 调度，可把多路 CPU 的有效带宽从跨 socket 争用中解放出来，且对主流 Transformer 变体透明、无需改模型代码。工程上为 LLM 推理服务在纯 CPU/无加速器或多路 CPU 节点上的 prefill（长 prompt、RAG 预计算、离线批量推理）提供了一个低成本、易部署的优化路径，也提示编译器后端（TorchInductor、XLA、TVM）应补齐拓扑感知的布局与切分决策。

Abstract: For sufficiently large transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs), whose parameters cannot be retained in on-chip caches, prefill throughput on multi-socket CPUs is often limited by sustainable DRAM bandwidth rather than peak floating-point throughput. In the standard PyTorch compilation (i.e., TorchInductor) path, model weights are allocated without considering the Non-Uniform Memory Access (NUMA) topology: specifically, the affinity between memory banks and compute cores is disregarded. This causes a substantial fraction of weight accesses to traverse the inter-socket interconnect, severely degrading the effective memory bandwidth available to each socket. We address this bottleneck through a compiler-level solution that makes TorchInductor NUMA-aware. Treating each NUMA socket as an independent tensor-parallel partition, we introduce NUMA-aware Tensor Parallelism (NUMA-TP). This approach transparently shards weights across sockets and schedules compute kernels such that thread pools and memory allocations remain strictly local to their respective sockets using CPU affinity. We evaluate the approach on GPT-2 (117M, 345M, 762M) and BERT (Small, Large), which exercise standard multi-head attention and MLP, as well as Gemma 3 270M, which combines Multi-Query Attention with a GeGLU-gated MLP. By strictly enforcing data locality, our approach successfully mitigates cross-socket traffic, achieving prefill speedups of up to 2.63x on a dual-socket Haswell platform, up to 1.87x on a dual-socket Cascade Lake platform, and up to 2.27x on a four-socket Haswell-EX platform.

### [2] [KOSMOS: Self-scheduling Persistent Kernels for Communication-Compute Overlap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07477)
*Alexander Magro,Aristotle Martin,Ye Wang,Wen Chen*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: KOSMOS 是一个"自调度持久化内核（self-scheduling persistent kernel）"库：把 TP+SP 的集合通信与 GEMM 融合、把整层 MoE（router 到 fold）融合成每个方向单次 launch，由 workgroup 依据"按消费者需求排序的队列"在通信与计算角色间动态切换，无需调度器、per-call 代价模型或预先调好的通信/计算切分比例。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: TP+SP 与 EP 的集合通信位于 transformer 层关键路径上。现有融合内核依赖固定的角色分配或静态调度（fixed roles / schedules），以及预先预测的通信-计算切分，因而在数据到达时间不齐时产生大量 idle window；预定的切分本质上是静态的，无法跟随数据 ready 状态动态变化。

Method: 核心机制：(1) 持久化内核 + workgroup 级自调度，以消费者需求（consumer demand）排序的工作队列驱动 workgroup 在通信角色与计算角色之间切换，去掉中央调度器与 per-call 代价模型；(2) 将 transformer 层内所有 TP+SP collective 与对应 GEMM 融合；(3) 将整个 expert layer（router → dispatch/combine → fold）按每一方向一次 launch 执行。评测平台为 AMD Instinct MI350X 与 MI355X。

Conclusion: 该工作说明：将通信-计算重叠的控制权从"离线调度/代价模型"下移到运行时数据就绪驱动的自调度，可系统性地消除融合内核的 idle window，并在 AMD GPU 上同时获得 TP+SP 与 EP 两条路径的端到端收益；代价是依赖持久化内核与队列式细粒度同步，对 SM/CU 占用与 kernel 内跨 workgroup 同步的正确性要求更高。

State-of-the-art fused kernels mitigate this with fixed roles or schedules, but this rigidity leaves large idle windows. Predicting a communication-computation split does not remove them either; a predetermined split cannot follow data readiness. KOSMOS fuses every TP+SP collective of a transformer layer with its GEMM, and runs an entire expert layer, from router to fold, in one launch per direction. Evaluated on AMD Instinct MI350X and AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs, KOSMOS speeds up TP+SP end-to-end training in Megatron-LM by 1.10-1.28x in BF16 and 1.10-1.25x in MXFP8. KOSMOS accelerates end-to-end training of DeepSeek-V3 and Qwen3-235B proxies by 1.29-1.83x over DeepEP.

### [3] [DMuon: Scaling Matrix Optimizers in Data Parallelism with Topology-Aware Matrix Ownership and Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07084)
*Starrick Liu,Ruizhi Chengze,Lufang Chen,Michael Zhao,Ran Yu,Xiaofan Li,dance kuzi,Yue Zhang,Zijuan Lin,SongAo Han,YongHua Chen,Ruyi Gan,Hao Wang*

Main category: cs.DC

TL;DR: DMuon 是一个面向 PyTorch FSDP(ZeRO-2/3)/HSDP 的矩阵粒度优化器运行时，通过"矩阵唯一 owner + 全矩阵本地更新 + 按计算量负载均衡"消除矩阵优化器（如 Muon）在参数分片下的额外通信与重复计算，并将由此产生的稀疏变长集合通信按就绪顺序调度、沿两级网络拓扑路由，在 256 张 H100 上达到 FSDP2+AdamW 中位数 1.05× 的吞吐，是 gather-then-replicate Muon 基线的 2.1×。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 矩阵级优化器（Muon 的 Newton–Schulz 迭代需要完整权重矩阵做正交化/矩阵符号运算）与数据并行运行时按元素/行分片（ZeRO-3 参数分片、HSDP 副本）天然冲突：参数一旦分片，Muon 就只能在每次 step 前 all-gather 完整矩阵（gather-then-replicate，通信与显存峰值翻倍），或在分片上重复执行 NS 迭代并用额外 all-reduce 修复一致性（重复计算 + 通信）。论文要解决的核心工程问题是：如何在不放弃 ZeRO-3 显存收益的前提下，让矩阵级优化器的通信与计算开销被"隐藏"而不是叠加。

Method: 1) **矩阵级所有权（owner）**：把每个权重矩阵整体分配给唯一 owner rank，由其在**未分片的全矩阵**上完成 NS 迭代与优化器更新，避免分片上的重复计算与跨 shard 的梯度拼接。2) **按计算代价负载均衡**：NS 迭代成本随矩阵形状/迭代步数变化，按估算算力而非参数量做 owner 分配，缓解矩阵大小不均导致的流水线气泡。3) **HSDP 全局所有权**：跨副本（replica group）统一分配 owner，使整个集群只保留**一份**梯度与优化器状态（ZeRO-2 风格），显存收益在 HSDP 下依然成立。4) **参数就绪即发布**：更新完的矩阵立即按分片广播到所有节点，与后续前向/反向计算 overlap，把 all-gather 藏在计算后面。5) **细粒度通信调度**：因所有权布局导致每次集合通信变成**稀疏、长度可变**的（只涉及某个矩阵的子集 rank、大小各异），DMuon 以"数据就绪即发起"的方式调度这些集合通信，并按集群**两级网络（节点内 NVLink / 节点间 IB）**做路由，减少小消息与不均衡消息的排队开销。

Result: 在最多 256 张 H100 上，覆盖 3 个稠密 LLM 与 2 个 VLA 模型：DMuon 的吞吐与 PyTorch FSDP2 + AdamW **持平或超过**，在 256 GPU 规模的中位数为 FSDP2+AdamW 的 **1.05×**；相对 gather-then-replicate Muon 基线，中位数吞吐为其 **2.1×**。即优化后的参数 gather 与梯度 reduce 完全吸收了矩阵优化器额外的通信和计算。

Conclusion: 论文的主要洞见是：矩阵优化器与分片运行时的矛盾不是"通信量"问题，而是**粒度错配**问题——把优化器执行粒度提升到矩阵、并让分片/通信围绕该粒度重新组织，就能在保留 ZeRO-3 显存收益的同时让 Muon 的 NS 开销被 overlap 掉，做到"矩阵优化器零额外税"。工程影响：(a) 为 FSDP2 ZeRO-2/3 与 HSDP 提供了一条可直接落地的 Muon 支持路径，替代目前社区常用的 gather-then-replicate 补丁；(b) "单副本优化器状态 + 全局 owner + 就绪即发布"对大规模 HSDP 有显存与大集群扩展性上的实际价值；(c) 稀疏变长集合通信的两级拓扑感知调度，是后续把更多非均匀并行模式（专家并行、异构分片、部分复制）接入 PyTorch 分布式栈的可复用机制；(d) 局限在于需要重写优化器/通信调度层、对 owner 负载建模较敏感，且 1.05× 的余量说明收益主要来自"不亏"，绝对加速需依赖更大模型与更多矩阵级优化器的采用。

Abstract: Matrix optimizers such as Muon update whole weight matrices, which conflicts with the element- or row-wise sharding of data-parallel runtimes. Once parameters are sharded as well, matrix optimizers incur extra communication or repeated computation. We present DMuon, a matrix-granular optimizer runtime for PyTorch FSDP (ZeRO-2/3) and HSDP. DMuon assigns each matrix a unique owner, which performs the Newton--Schulz (NS) iteration and optimizer update on the full matrix, and balances the load across owners by computational cost. On HSDP, ownership is global across replicas, so the cluster keeps a single copy of gradients and optimizer state, and updated parameters are published across nodes as soon as they are ready, overlapping subsequent computation. This layout makes every collective sparse and variable in length, so DMuon schedules these collectives as their data becomes ready and routes them along the cluster's two-level network. On up to 256 H100 GPUs, across three dense LLMs and two VLA models, DMuon matches or exceeds the training throughput of PyTorch FSDP2 with AdamW, reaching 1.05$\times$ that of FSDP2 with AdamW at the median on 256 GPUs. The optimized parameter gathering and gradient reduction absorb the extra communication and computation a matrix optimizer requires. Against a gather-then-replicate Muon baseline, DMuon's throughput is 2.1 times as high at the median.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Experiments show that AFD has a throughput advantage of up to 1.17x over wide EP on Qwen3 and 1.16x on Qwen3.5, and matches it on MiniMax-M2.5. This view explains which models benefit from AFD, and shows that speculative decoding helps light-expert models most, where the enlarged overlap gain outweighs the larger disaggregation cost and adds a further 8% to AFD's advantage over wide EP. Looking ahead, serving the experts on accelerators that keep their weights in on-chip SRAM, such as the Groq LPX accelerators paired with Vera Rubin NVL72, would remove most of the disaggregation cost, raising the ideal speedup to 2.48x with speculative decoding. FastAFD is open source at https://github.com/hao-ai-lab/FastAFD.
